## Community

Why: Discusses multiple genres of games, consoles, gaming news, industry trends, and player experiences. It isn't limited to one game or release, so posts cover a variety of topics.

## Posts I read

Read 40 posts on 10/14/2026. Skimmed: hot.

## What I noticed

- Some posts sharing trailers or announcements include a short overview or personal thoughts, while others mainly share the link or video.
- Some posts mention specific details, such as game titles, release dates, prices, or developers. Others make broader statements without concrete details.
- Some posts include personal experiences or opinions about a game, while others focus on reporting news or sharing information.
- Some posts raise questions or invite discussion, while others are primarily announcements, memes, screenshots, or standalone opinions.

## Candidate distinctions (things I can point at)

1. Some posts that share trailers or announcements include a short overview or personal thoughts. Others provide little or no commentary beyond the shared content.
2. Some posts mention concrete details, such as a release date, price, game mechanic, or developer. Most other posts focus on general reactions or opinions.
3. Some posts explain *why* the author likes or dislikes something, using examples or personal experiences. Others state an opinion without explaining it.
4. Some posts invite meaningful discussion through a question or debatable claim. Others share content without giving readers much to respond to.

## Open questions

- How much commentary is enough to distinguish a post with an explanation from one that simply shares content?
- Should a post containing a detailed personal opinion count as evidence-based if it doesn't cite external sources?
- How should memes and screenshots be categorized when they generate substantial discussion but contain little written text?
- Are posts with specific details necessarily more substantial, or can a general discussion post be equally valuable?
